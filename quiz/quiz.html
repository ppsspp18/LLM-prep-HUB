How to Use Quiz Mode

  1. Enter your target Topic, Concept, pick a difficulty, and choose question count.
  2. Click 📋 Copy Prompt and paste it into any LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini).
  3. Copy the JSON response from the LLM and paste it into the Paste Quiz JSON box.
  4. Click Generate Quiz, solve the questions, and click Submit Quiz.
  5. Click 📋 Copy Concept & Knowledge Gap Prompt to get a single unified explanation of the forgotten concepts from the LLM.
