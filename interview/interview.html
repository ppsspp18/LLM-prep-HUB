How to Use Interview Mode

  1. Fill in your desired Topic, Concept, Difficulty, and number of questions.
  2. Click 📋 Copy Prompt and paste it into any LLM to generate interview questions JSON.
  3. Paste the resulting JSON into the box below and click Generate Interview Questions.
  4. Write your answers to each open-ended question.
  5. Click 📋 Copy Feedback Prompt, paste it into the LLM to grade your answers, then paste the returned feedback JSON to view detailed AI scores and feedback.
